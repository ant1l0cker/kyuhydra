FAQ

Questions applicants ask us.

Do I need an account to apply?

Yes. A free account lets you apply to roles, track every application from your dashboard and see its status in one place.

What happens after I submit?

Your application moves to Under review. A member of our team reads your profile and assessment answers, and we reach out within 48 hours.

Why do you ask for ID verification?

We verify identity so employers can trust the people they meet. Your ID is used only for verification and the name must match your profile.

Are the assessments scored automatically?

No. Multiple-choice and written answers are reviewed by our team, so please answer in your own words.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes. Apply to as many roles as fit your experience. Each application appears separately on your dashboard.

Are these employee or contractor roles?

Each listing states whether it is full-time, part-time or contract. Contractor engagements can be extended, shortened or concluded early depending on needs and performance.
